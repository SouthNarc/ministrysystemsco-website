INSIDE THE MAIN GUIDE

From network audit to a Sunday-ready plan.

Understand and improve your setup

  • Audit the existing network before buying more equipment.
  • Distinguish internet service, wired connections, and Wi-Fi problems.
  • Map the building and plan access-point placement.
  • Separate staff, guest, production, and facilities traffic.

Prepare your team to respond

  • Protect livestream and other ministry-critical services.
  • Test under real Sunday conditions and troubleshoot systematically.
  • Build an outage plan, network records, and volunteer roles.
  • Work through a 30-day improvement plan.
