A clear path from assessment to implementation.

  1. Introduction: What Makes a Livestream Sunday-ProofThe principles behind a reliable, repeatable, volunteer-friendly system.
  2. Start Here: The Livestream AuditEvaluate video, audio, computer, network, volunteers, and documentation.

  10. Your 30-Day Sunday-Proof Implementation PlanTurn the guide into a staged plan for improving your livestream ministry.

“Sunday-Proof” means preparing your team to handle ordinary problems. It doesn’t mean technology can never fail. The goal is a simpler, more sustainable ministry workflow.
